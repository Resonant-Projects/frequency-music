# Feedback: Everything Is a Resonant Body

## Overall Impression
This essay is a fantastic synthesis of physical acoustics, deep learning architecture, and musical composition. By framing rooms, faces, neuromorphic chips, and neural networks all as "resonant bodies," you provide a unified theory of audio processing that is both scientifically accurate and compositionally inspiring.

## Structure and Argument
The progression through the four examples (Room, Face, Chip, Network) is perfectly structured. You move from the literal and obvious (a room's impulse response) to the biological (the vocal tract and face) to the computational (reservoir computing) to the abstract (a neural network's learned weights). 

The connection between Alvin Lucier's *I Am Sitting in a Room* and digital convolution is a great anchor for the "Transfer Function as Compositional Unit" section.

## Clarity and Flow
The essay is highly readable. The definition of resonance ("Memory + periodic input = selective amplification = resonance") is brilliant in its simplicity and accuracy. 

## Style and Voice
The tone is authoritative, interdisciplinary, and practical. It bridges the gap between DSP engineering and avant-garde composition effortlessly.
