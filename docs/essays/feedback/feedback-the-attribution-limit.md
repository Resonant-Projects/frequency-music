# Feedback: The Attribution Limit

## Overall Impression

This essay examines how attribution limits constrain what can be identified and recognized within audio signals, connecting technical research from recent extraction clusters to practical compositional applications. It argues that understanding attribution limits is essential for composers who work with complex or layered sounds, as these limits determine which musical features remain perceivable and actionable.

## Structure and Argument

The essay follows a clear progression:
1. **Introduction**: Establishes the relationship between attribution limits and musical perception
2. **Technical Analysis**: Explores how attribution limits function in audio processing systems
3. **Compositional Applications**: Demonstrates how attribution limit insights can guide musical decisions
4. **Conclusion**: Synthesizes the key findings and their implications

Each section builds on the previous one, creating a comprehensive argument about the importance of attribution limits for composers.

## Clarity and Flow

The essay maintains clear, analytical prose while covering complex technical concepts. The transitions between sections are smooth, and the progression from theory to practice is well-structured.

## Style and Voice

The voice is precise and analytical, with appropriate emphasis on practical applications. The essay successfully bridges the gap between technical research and musical practice.
