# Feedback: The Bandwidth of a Musical Instruction

## Overall Impression

This essay has a strong practical premise: a compositional rule matters perceptually only if some carrier makes it available soon enough to guide expectation or action. The storage/transmission distinction and the final emphasis on “timely recoverability” are useful. The essay also responsibly says that “instruction bandwidth” is not a universal Shannon measure.

That disclaimer exposes the central problem, however: if bandwidth is neither a rate nor a capacity that can be compared under stated conditions, the term risks becoming a technical-sounding synonym for perceptual legibility. The definition combines at least four variables—amount of rule, recoverability, latency, and foreground salience—without saying how they trade off. Decide whether the proposal is primarily a diagnostic question (“can the rule be recovered by deadline D?”) or a comparative quantity. If comparative, name a task, decoder/listener population, error threshold, channel transformation, and observation window. Otherwise use “instruction availability” or explicitly frame bandwidth as a heuristic metaphor throughout.

## Structure and Argument

The essay’s sequence is sensible: define the term, separate storage from transmission, specify carriers and windows, introduce multiplexing, then test the idea. Yet much of “Every Carrier Has a Different Instruction Budget” repeats “The Decoding Window,” and “Relation Multiplexing” imports the separate rank-budget framework before the present concept is secure. Compress those sections and use the saved space to work one rule through an entire signal path.

The watermark analogy needs more care. A cryptographic payload recovered by a machine decoder is not analogous in every relevant way to a tacit stylistic rule recognized by a listener. One has an explicit code and bit-error criterion; the other may emerge through statistical learning and may never enter awareness. State the limited transfer: both require a carrier, transformations, and a recovery criterion, but only the watermark supplies literal channel capacity. Also verify the named “Asymmetric Phase Coding” implementation and robustness claims with a primary source; “survive compression, resampling, filtering, and cropping” is too categorical without attack parameters and success rates.

The essay says an instruction can remain “below foreground perception and still be recoverable,” but a watermark is recoverable to a suitable algorithm, not necessarily musically operative for a human. That distinction threatens the definition, which alternates among listeners, performers, and decoders. Define the intended recipient each time.

The studio study cannot support the stated hypothesis as written. Version B alters spectrum, Version C alters duration/order, and Version D translates the rule; these introduce unequal transformations. “Blind-rate” is also unclear because questions such as “Can the response rule be described?” require coding open-ended answers. Use prediction accuracy and time-to-criterion for each declared rule, plus a separate identity judgment. Control intelligibility and generic fidelity rather than merely loudness.

## Clarity and Flow

“Instruction,” “rule,” “identity,” “structure,” and “relation” often substitute for one another. A voicing habit may cue agent identity without instructing any next action; a spectral signature can be an identifier rather than a rule. Define instruction narrowly as evidence that changes a recipient’s prediction or permitted action. Then classify descriptive signatures separately.

The carrier examples are vivid, but several are asserted rather than demonstrated: a distorted bass does not necessarily erase inner-voice motion, a 600 ms chunk does not necessarily conceal slow form, and narrowband treatment does not invariably remove source identity. Conditional language would improve factual precision without weakening the compositional point.

## Style and Voice

The controlled, aphoristic voice suits the subject, especially “Storage is the easy half.” Still, the essay has too many coined labels—embedded instruction, latent score, instruction bandwidth, effective instruction, decoding window, relation multiplexing, relational rank, rank budget. The terminology begins to compete for the very bandwidth under discussion. Retain the title concept and decoding window; present multiplexing as a technique rather than another formal construct.

The conclusion is attractive but absolute: musical instructions need not “reach the listener before the moment” if retrospective reinterpretation is the intended effect. Revise the ending to allow both prospective and retrospective functions.

## Line-Level Edits

- “amount of latent musical rule” is not operational. Try: “the degree to which a specified listener or decoder can recover a rule, at a stated accuracy, before a stated deadline.”
- “A rule may be present yet have effectively zero instruction bandwidth” should become “A rule may be encoded yet remain unavailable to the intended recipient under the actual listening conditions.”
- “Redundancy and error correction allow the signature to survive…” should be qualified: “The cited method uses redundancy and error correction to improve recovery after tested transformations such as…”
- “The same musical phrase therefore has no single instruction bandwidth” confuses phrase and channel. Try: “Recoverability is relative to the phrase’s encoding, channel, recipient, and task.”
- “A 600 ms chunking process may make local call-and-response highly legible” needs explanation or evidence; chunk size alone does not create legibility.
- “The remaining four are not ‘deep’ merely because they are hidden” is sharp but combative. Try: “Hidden relations should not be credited as operative unless the intended recipient can recover them.”
- “A musical instruction succeeds when it reaches the listener before the moment in which it needs to matter” should become “It succeeds prospectively when it guides the relevant decision in time, or retrospectively when later evidence productively revises that decision.”
