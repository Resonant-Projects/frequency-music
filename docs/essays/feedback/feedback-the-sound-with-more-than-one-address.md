# Feedback: The Sound With More Than One Address

## Overall Impression

“Address” is a productive metaphor for compositional control, and the essay’s studio exercise is concrete enough to justify it. The synthesis nevertheless slips between perceptual interpretation, neural representation, and geometric indexing as though all were operational layers of the same sound. They are not yet shown to be commensurable. The revision should define addressability in terms of an agent, operation, and parameter, and it should verify the unusually specific technical claims about multipitch perception and lattice-counting room simulation.

## Structure and Argument

The opening thesis—“A sound becomes composable when it can be addressed at more than one operational layer”—is too absolute. Acoustic instruments have always been composable without independent access to every perceptual or physical variable. The defensible claim is that additional independently controllable representations expand compositional possibilities.

The three cases currently share only that something can be located or manipulated. In Vitalic, an “address” is a listener’s perceived pitch stream or a spectral partial group. In the audio-language model, it is an internal frame representation accessed by a trained head. In room simulation, it is a mathematical image-source coordinate. Explain who addresses each layer and what operation becomes possible. Without that test, “address” risks meaning any representation whatsoever.

The Vitalic discussion needs citations and acoustic caution. A single inharmonic complex tone may evoke more than one virtual pitch, but “two simultaneous melodies” across a line is a strong perceptual claim. State whether the paper reports listening evidence, transcription analysis, or author interpretation. “Changing one partial group” independently may not preserve the asserted percept; present it as an experiment, not a clean consequence.

The temporal-localization account also needs precision. What model, task, and comparison support “acts sooner”? A frame-level head can avoid autoregressive timestamp tokens, but lower latency does not follow unless measured. The room section is the shakiest: Gauss-circle counting generally counts lattice points; it does not necessarily retain individual reflection addresses, incorporate boundaries and frequency-dependent absorption exactly, or replace image-source enumeration for rendering. The phrase “connected across dimensions by convolution” needs explanation or removal.

The ending repeats the thesis through “address,” “layer,” and “handles.” Let the studio exercise be the proof of usefulness, then close on the criterion of independent audible change.

## Clarity and Flow

Define “operational layer” and “address” immediately. A useful formula might be: an address is a representation that lets a specified user or system select and vary one property while monitoring what remains invariant. This would sharpen “one sound,” which alternately refers to waveform, event, representation, and percept.

“Event intensity,” “probability field,” “reflection density,” and “lattice weighting” are introduced without enough mechanics to let readers judge the proposed tools. Brief definitions would prevent suggestive phrases from masquerading as available controls.

## Style and Voice

The voice is lively and the modular headings suit the three-case design. The repeated sentence pattern “The X extraction says something similar” smooths the prose but understates differences that should drive the argument. Use contrast as well as analogy.

The linked “Implicit Evidence” aside feels like graph maintenance rather than essay development. Either summarize the exact conceptual dependency in a sentence or leave the link to the connections note.

## Line-Level Edits

- “A sound becomes composable when…” → “A sound offers new compositional possibilities when more than one of its properties can be independently addressed.”
- “The waveform gives the ear more than one pitch address.” → “The paper argues that one inharmonic complex supports two concurrent pitch interpretations.”
- “The time of an event is already latent in the frames.” Replace “is” with “may be decodable”: probe success does not establish a neatly localized latent variable.
- “The system acts sooner” → “The design could reduce dependence on autoregressive timestamp emission; report measured latency before claiming earlier action.”
- “Every reflection corresponds to a mirrored source.” Qualify by model: “In the idealized image-source model, specular reflection paths correspond to mirrored sources.”
- “A room can be a harmonic field” risks confusing acoustical geometry with harmonic pitch relations. Consider “a structured field of possible returns.”
- “The more independently those addresses can be moved…” → “The more independently those parameters can be varied while producing audible, stable distinctions…”
- In the exercise, replace “proves the difference” with “demonstrates an audible difference,” and ask how that difference will be evaluated.
