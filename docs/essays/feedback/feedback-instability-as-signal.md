# Feedback — "Instability as Signal"

## Overall Impression
Interesting frame — instability as a source of information rather than a defect to suppress — and the three-part taxonomy (bodily / computational / generative) is a genuine contribution. The essay is at its best when it holds the ethical worry about elderly-speech data alongside the technical possibilities. It's weakest when it slips into "compositional handle" listing mode toward the end.

## Structure / Argument
- The three-source pairing is well-chosen and the taxonomy that falls out of it is useful.
- "The Fragility Test" is the most original section. The idea of using inference-time precision variation as a compositional probe is genuinely new territory for this series. Extend it — describe what an implementation could look like.
- The "Tremor Is Not Noise" section handles the ethical dimension responsibly. The turn from "controls" to "not imitating a demographic identity" is important and correctly placed.
- "Generating the Edge" is the weakest section because the zero-shot benchmark is the thinnest of the three sources here; the essay leans on it to complete the taxonomy but the source doesn't quite bear the weight.
- The studio exercise ties the three layers together well but the "assign each layer a musical role" phase is too quick — it deserves a paragraph, not a sentence.

## Clarity / Flow
- Well-organized.
- "The model is not a judge but a resonator" is a strong reframing.
- The essay does not include a source-ID appendix at the top level and its "Related" line is inline; this is cleaner than the format used in earlier essays. Keep this format.

## Style / Voice
- Consistent. Slightly less inflected than adjacent essays, which is fine.
- "Stable instability" as a phrase is doing a lot of work; it's memorable but risks being too clever if used again elsewhere.

## Line-Level Notes
- "The hard part of the signal is not random damage. It is a patterned departure from the training center." — sharp.
- "Treat the body's variation with care. Treat the model's variation as a probe. Treat the generator's variation as a space." — good rhetorical rhythm; also happens to be the essay's actual proposal condensed.
- "flaky speech recognizer" — a little too colloquial for the surrounding register.
- The move from tremor as first-class control to "abstract contour of tremor depth" applied to filter motion is the most concrete compositional proposal in the piece. Give it a full paragraph.
