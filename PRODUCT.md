# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is the owner-researcher and composer steering the full research-to-composition loop. A small number of collaborators contribute feedback and draft material, while followers inspect published research. In the app, the primary job is deciding what deserves attention next without losing provenance or overstating evidence.

## Product Purpose

Frequency Music turns research sources into claims, cross-domain correspondences, testable musical hypotheses, recipes, compositions, and structured listening feedback. Success means the operator can move from evidence to an audible experiment, see where the loop is blocked or accumulating debt, and make the next human judgment with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, contemplative, craft-focused. The voice should respect uncertainty while keeping the musical and perceptual stakes tangible.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, decorative mysticism that obscures evidence, and clinical research interfaces that erase the embodied studio practice. Do not use visual drama to imply certainty the underlying data does not support.

## Design Principles

- Make provenance and computed facts easier to trust than generated narrative.
- Put the next human judgment where the operator naturally completes the loop.
- Preserve musical meaning alongside procedural and technical detail.
- Use emphasis sparingly so blocked work and irreversible decisions earn attention.
- Keep legacy records understandable as the system evolves.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic controls, visible keyboard focus, meaningful live-region feedback, and text contrast suitable for sustained reading. Do not rely on color alone for state. Respect reduced-motion preferences whenever motion is introduced.
