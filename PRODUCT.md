## Platform

web

## Product purpose

Frequency connects research to musical experiments through sources, extracted claims, concepts, hypotheses, recipes, compositions, and listening feedback. The repository's domain contracts and terminology remain authoritative.

## Home exploration

The user confirmed that the 3D home should prioritize selecting anything and tracing its related concepts and research. Preserve the astrolabe identity and research-to-composition workflow. The home must explain recorded relationships and provide readable navigation alongside spatial interaction.

## Capabilities and constraints

The web application uses Solid, Three.js, and Convex subscriptions. The current home queries return bounded samples; do not present that sample as the entire archive. Counts and relationships must come from data. Spatial grouping alone is not evidence of a relationship. Reduced motion, keyboard navigation, and usable navigation when WebGL is unavailable are required by the existing design system.

## Evidence on hand

DESIGN.md and web/docs/zodiac-style-guide.md document the established visual identity. GLOSSARY.md and convex/schema.ts define the domain. Product doctrine lives in docs/vision-and-meaning.md.

The home has only the spatial view. Search results and domain concepts remain keyboard-accessible in its inspector. Escape clears the current selection, history, and search, and resets the camera. Workflow navigation belongs in the shared top navigation.
