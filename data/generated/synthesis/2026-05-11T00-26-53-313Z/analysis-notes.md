## Connection Found: The Nearest Accessible Form

Across this batch, several sources distinguish theoretical possibility from reachable transition:

- S4 (Tonnetz/combinatorial geometry) formalizes harmonic materials as graphs/configurations, making "next chord" a question of graph adjacency rather than abstract availability.
- S3 (speech enhancement probing) separates stable encoder representations from adaptive decoder layers, suggesting transformations can preserve an invariant core while adapting at the reconstruction boundary.
- S2 (Qwen3.5-Omni/ARIA) shows instability when text and speech units are misaligned, and stabilization by dynamic alignment.
- S5 (EDM localization) reduces an intractable spatial search by choosing a representation whose constraints make the solution reachable.
- S6 (David Mayer) gives a production-language version: call-and-response creates a chain of plausible answers around an emotional/demo anchor.

The compelling synthesis is that musical transitions should be evaluated not only by destination quality but by path accessibility. A strong arrangement makes each intermediate state feel like the nearest reachable form under the current harmonic, timbral, spatial, and expressive constraints.
