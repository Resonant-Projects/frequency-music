# Analysis Notes - The Uncertain Edge

## Connection

The strongest cross-source pattern is that boundaries become usable when uncertainty is represented rather than collapsed:

- S1: ice phases move through nearby, path-dependent metastable states.
- S2: streaming speech needs dynamic alignment between symbolic and acoustic token clocks.
- S3: SSL speech features expose partly separable acoustic axes, so a voice can clarify dimension by dimension.
- S4: phoneme boundaries are gradient regions expressible as confidence intervals.
- S5: video-to-music generation separates global latent planning from local audio rendering.
- S6: rare acoustic events can be learned from sparse raw audio with millisecond event annotation.

## Compositional Translation

Treat a transition not as an edit point but as a measured region whose internal ordering can be composed. Useful variables: boundary width, which dimension clarifies first, whether a metastable plateau appears, whether symbolic and acoustic clocks align, and whether rare events become detectable against a learned background.

## Risk

The idea becomes weak if it is reduced to generic ambience, haze, or crossfading. The test needs explicit controls: hard cuts or uniform crossfades between the same source states.
