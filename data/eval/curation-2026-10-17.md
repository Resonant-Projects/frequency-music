## Ratifying (after Keith ticks the boxes)

The golden files are generated by
`scripts/langsmith/materialize-curation-2026-07-18.ts`, and a hand edit is
reverted on the next run, so ratification goes through the materializer:

1. Record the decision (accepted ids, H4 choice) at the top of this sheet.
2. Add the accepted ids to `HYPOTHESIS_IDS` in the materializer, with a
   comment naming this sheet. If H4 is dropped, remove its id. This edits
   `scripts/langsmith/`, so it needs its own authorization. Plan 008 forbids
   the sweep from editing that directory.
3. Run the materializer. It reads production through read-only Convex queries
   and rewrites **all three** golden files, so it needs the parent's
   authorization for a production read.
4. Run the drift check in the
   [readiness runbook](../../docs/evidence/eval-baseline-20261017/readiness.md#ratification-and-drift-check).
   It compares every upload key of every regenerated row with this approved
   snapshot: the golden files at `57f7c90` plus the proposed file. It ignores
   the `sources` and `thesis` fields the materializer adds, because the upload
   reads neither. Any changed or unknown row goes back to Keith before upload.
5. Approve the upload by the regenerated files' hashes, not by the hashes of
   this snapshot.
