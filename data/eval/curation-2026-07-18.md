## How to use this

1. Read down the tables. Every row has a recommendation (**KEEP** / **SKIP**) and
   a one-phrase rationale. Tick the box to accept the recommendation; untick to
   overrule it.
2. Ratify the pass by recording the accepted `_id`s in
   `scripts/langsmith/materialize-curation-2026-07-18.ts` — `EXTRACTION_IDS`,
   `HYPOTHESIS_IDS`, `WEEKLY_BRIEF_IDS` — plus any per-row correction in
   `HYPOTHESIS_EXTRACTION_REPOINTS`.
3. Run `vpx tsx scripts/langsmith/materialize-curation-2026-07-18.ts` to write
   `extractions-golden.jsonl`, `hypotheses-golden.jsonl`, and
   `weekly-briefs-golden.jsonl` (see `README.md` § Row shape).
4. Then run the upload + baseline commands in the footer.

Quality criteria are `docs/agent-tool-surface.md` § Dataset Quality Criteria.

The golden files are **generated**, not hand-edited — the script is the single
source of truth, so a manual edit is silently reverted on the next run. Every
curation decision belongs in the script, and the sheet records why.

> Claim 4 says an 8 kHz low-pass on 44.1 kHz audio "eliminates more than 80% of
> the representable frequency spectrum". Nyquist is 22.05 kHz, so it removes
> ≈63.7%, not >80%. The claim carries `evidenceLevel: "personal"` and the
> `rationaleMd` repeats the figure.
>
> Not corrected here, because it is a curation decision with two defensible
> answers, and the golden files are generated: (a) leave it, on the grounds that
> a golden *extraction* should faithfully mirror what the source asserted, errors
> included — that is what makes it a fair test of extraction fidelity; or (b)
> drop/replace H4, because `hypotheses-golden.jsonl` is scored as reference
> *output*, so a wrong figure in `rationaleMd` teaches the eval to reward it.
>
> If (b): re-run the source extraction rather than hand-editing the JSONL, since
> the materialization script overwrites it.
